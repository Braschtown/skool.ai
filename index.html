Draft due dates matter — teachers give feedback that improves the final grade. Final due dates are firm; extensions must be requested from the school before the due date. Card positions sync live for the whole family. Removed a subject you don't take? Use the × on the card — it hides it for everyone, and you can bring it back any time from "Removed" in the menu. Tap the flag on a card to mark it for follow-up, with an optional note, visible to everyone on the board. If a due date changes, tap it to correct it (parent PIN required) — a small pencil shows on any date that's been adjusted from the original.
